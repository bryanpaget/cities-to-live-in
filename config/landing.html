About This Presentation

A policy manifesto for rebuilding cities around human life, with a national bike share as backbone infrastructure. It covers:

  • The city model: villages and neighborhoods where everything daily is a 15-minute walk
  • The movement hierarchy: walking, cycling and scooters first; cars and trucks as guests
  • The bike share as a national public utility, not a private app
  • Safe routes to school and the retooling of policing around children
  • A phased nationwide rollout, and the anti-patterns that kill half-measures
